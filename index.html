Privacy Policy for Nour

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy applies to the Nour app on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

Who we are

Nour is developed and published by an individual developer under the name Laskoy Labs.
Contact: laskoylabs@gmail.com

What data we collect

  • Anonymous account identifier: When you first open the app, we create an anonymous account for you (Firebase Authentication anonymous sign-in). There is no sign-up, and this identifier is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Approximate location (optional): If you choose to share your location, we use it only for prayer times and prayer-time notifications. On iOS, coordinates are rounded to about 1 km before they are stored or sent anywhere, and prayer times are calculated on your device. Your coordinates may be sent to the Aladhan API (to retrieve prayer times or the Hijri date) and to your device platform's location service (Apple or Google) to look up the name of a place or city. We do not store your location on our servers. You can decline location sharing and pick a city instead, or use Mecca times.
  • Profile information you provide (optional): your name, city/country, gender and stated intent, entered during onboarding or in Settings. Your name and gender are sent with your chat messages so Nour can address you appropriately.
  • Chat messages and shared content: Messages you send to Nour, and any video links you share for reflection, are sent to our backend and stored in our database (Firebase Firestore) so your conversation history can be shown back to you.
  • Subscription information: If you subscribe to Nour Pro, the purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We receive the transaction details (for example, which plan and until when it is active) and send them to our backend to verify your subscription and unlock Pro features. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Usage data: Basic app analytics (Firebase Analytics) to understand how features are used, and crash reports to fix problems.

Your reading progress, favorite verses, intentions and settings are stored only on your device.

How we use your data

  • To generate Nour's replies and video reflections.
  • To show you your own chat history.
  • To verify your subscription and enforce usage limits.
  • To calculate prayer times and send prayer-time notifications.
  • To understand usage and improve the app.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Third-party processors

To provide the app's features, some data is processed by:

  • DeepSeek: generates Nour's replies and video reflections from your messages (and your name and gender, if you provided them). DeepSeek may process data outside your country.
  • Firebase (Google): authentication, database (Firestore) and analytics.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play): payments and subscription management, and on-device services such as place-name lookup.
  • Aladhan API: prayer times and the Hijri date, based on the city or approximate coordinates you provide.
  • Text-to-speech service: Noor Story audio is generated on our server from the day's verse and reflection question. No personal data is sent for this.
  • quran.com and alquran.cloud: Quran text, translations, transliteration and recitations. No personal data is sent to them.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy terms. We send only what is needed for the requested feature.

Data retention and deletion

Chat history is kept so you can revisit past conversations.

To delete your data: open Nour, go to Settings → Delete Account (on Android under Settings → Account) and confirm. This permanently and immediately deletes your chat history, saved preferences and usage records from our database (Firebase Firestore), along with your anonymous account. This cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; manage that in your store account settings.

If you can't use the in-app option, email laskoylabs@gmail.com with any details that identify your use of the app, and we will delete your associated records within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Nour is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country's law), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

AI and sensitive content notice

Nour's companion is an AI. It can make mistakes, and its replies are not a fatwa or a religious ruling. Nour is not a medical, psychological or emergency service. If you are experiencing a mental-health crisis, please contact a licensed professional, a crisis helpline in your country, or emergency services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
